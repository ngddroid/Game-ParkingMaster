Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · Applies to Inch Perfect: Car Parking

We don't collect, store or share any of your personal data. Your progress stays on your device.

01What we collect

Nothing personal. The Game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any account. It doesn't use analytics or tracking SDKs and doesn't need an internet connection.

02What stays on your device

  • Your level progress, stars and best times
  • Coins, purchased items, garage customisation and boosters
  • Settings such as volume, controls and graphics quality
  • Gameplay statistics shown on the Stats screen

This data is stored privately in the Game's storage on your device. We can't see it. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes it. Android's own device backup may include it, under your Google account settings.

03Permissions

  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can switch off in Settings.
  • Motion sensor: only read when you choose the "Tilt to Steer" control scheme. It's processed instantly and never stored.

04Advertising (future)

This version shows no ads. If optional rewarded ads are added later, an advertising partner may process device identifiers under its own privacy policy. We'll update this policy and the Play Store data safety section before that happens.

05Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

06Your choices

You can erase all game data at any time under Settings → Reset Progress, or by clearing the app's storage in Android settings.

07Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the Game under Settings → Privacy, with a new effective date.

08Contact

Privacy questions? Contact NG Studio at support@example.com.
